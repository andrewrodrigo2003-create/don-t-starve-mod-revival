BETTER LUCY - DEVOTED WIFE STYLE
================================

This personality expansion is original dialogue inspired by a private couple's
casual texting rhythm. No names, workplaces, identifying details, or private
conversation transcripts are included in the mod.

Style notes:
- short/direct affectionate phrasing
- shy text faces like v///v and >.< used selectively
- sweet -> blunt -> teasing -> possessive rhythm
- marriage/wife loyalty
- suggestive double-entendres without explicit sexual description
- occasional assertive/rough-play jokes framed as playful axe/tree humor
